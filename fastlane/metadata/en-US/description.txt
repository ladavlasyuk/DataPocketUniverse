Pocket Universe is a calm and creative cosmic sandbox where you can shape your own miniature universe without missions, scores, or fixed objectives. Start with a tiny star surrounded by infinite darkness and use intuitive touch controls to create and manipulate matter, stars, planets, and other cosmic objects. Tap to scatter matter, hold to create new stars, swipe to influence cosmic drift, pinch to adjust gravity, and shake your device to trigger spontaneous cosmic events. Real-time physics allow objects to interact naturally through movement, gravity, and collisions, creating unique outcomes in every session. Explore five cosmic sectors, each featuring its own gravity settings, visual palette, and limitations, and experiment with different formations such as planetary systems, binary stars, or collapsing black holes. Save your creations as collectible universe snapshot cards and track statistics including stars, planets, collisions, and lifetime activity. Customize your observer profile with a name and portrait, adjust sound and haptic feedback, and enjoy a distraction-free portrait experience with atmospheric deep-space visuals and subtle animations. Designed for peaceful creative play, Pocket Universe offers a relaxing space for experimentation, discovery, and emergent cosmic interactions.
